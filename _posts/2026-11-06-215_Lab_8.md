# DNA Barcoding Lab
We are now going to extract DNA, perform PCR, and sequence the _rbcL_ barcode region for the mosses we collected. 

## Learning Objectives
- Learn about the uses of DNA barcoding
- Learn how to extract DNA
- Learn how to perform polymerase chain reaction
- Learn how to perform gel electrophoresis
- Learn about nanopore sequencing
- Learn how to use NCBI GenBank and BLAST

## Pre-Lab
1. Watch how to [extract DNA from plants](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ucS32uz7LPg&t=3011s)
2. Watch how to [perform PCR and gel electrophoresis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cZgeHdEUunE)
3. Watch about [nanopore sequencing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VxGliKyYuFQ)
4. Read the [protocol](https://dnalc.cshl.edu/assets/files/dnalc-live/17102.dna_barcoding_protocol.pdf)

## Lab Procedures
1. Retrieve your moss from the herbarium.
2. [Extract DNA.](https://dnalc.cshl.edu/assets/files/dnalc-live/17102.dna_barcoding_protocol.pdf)
3. [Perform PCR.](https://dnalc.cshl.edu/assets/files/dnalc-live/17102.dna_barcoding_protocol.pdf)
4. In a group of 8, [run a gel.](https://dnalc.cshl.edu/assets/files/dnalc-live/17102.dna_barcoding_protocol.pdf)
5. Visual the gel. Print the photo.
6. If your PCR was successful save the PCR product for sequencing.

## Assessment
1. Show me the gel for 10 points.
2. If your PCR was successful, you get 3 extra credit points.
