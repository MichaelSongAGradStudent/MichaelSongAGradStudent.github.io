# Plant Diversity Lab
In this lab, we will be learning about plant diversity and how to infer a phylogeny from morphological characters. 

## Learning Objectives
- Learn the 7 major plant phyla
- Learn 12 major Angiosperm families
- Learn the haplodiplontic life cycle

## Lab Procedures
- Look and draw the following organisms, note the killer synapomorphies for each taxon. Note the gametophyte and sporophyte, if applicable. Note whether homo- or heterosporous and if di- or monoecious. Note the locations of meiosis and the ploidy of each structure.
    1. Mosses
    2. Hornworts
    3. Leafy Liverworts
    4. Thalloid Liverworts
    5. _Selaginella_
    6. _Lycopodium_/_Huperzia_
    7. Salviniales
    8. _Equisetum_
    9. Pteridaceae
    10. Eupolypod I ferns (Polypodiineae) 
    11. Eupolypod II ferns (Aspleniineae)
    12. _Ginkgo_
    13. _Cycas_/_Dioon_
    14. Cupressaceae
    15. Pinaceae
    16. Taxaceae
    17. Asteraceae
    18. Ericaceae
    19. Brassicaceae
    20. Liliales/Asparagales
    21. Rosaceae
    22. Caryophyllales
    23. Poaceae
    24. Lamiaceae
    25. Fabaceae
    26. Malvaceae
    27. Solanaceae
    28. Papaveraceae
 - Make a dichotomous key to identify these 28 taxa
 - We will now infer a phylogeny
 - Make a character matrix [Example](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1oDbHIwcD2RtgZTlZExh13k1SJfjmoqhKp-QZXoYZOSk/edit?usp=sharing) of these 20 taxa in a google sheet, coded for 12 characters (e.g. sporophyte vs gametophyte dominant, homosporous vs heterosporous, fruit absence vs presence, flower absence vs presence, etc.
 - Transform that character matrix into a nexus file format, [Example](https://revbayes.github.io/tutorials/morph_tree/data/bears.nex)
 - Download [Paup](https://paup.phylosolutions.com/get-paup/) and then run Paup* and infer a neighbor joining tree:

```execute YOUR_MATRIX.nex```
   
  ```set criterion=distance;```
  
  ```set root=outgroup;```
  
  ```set storebrlens=yes;```
  
  ```taxset my_outgroups = CHOOSE YOUR OUTGROUP```
  
  ```outgroup my_outgroups;```
  
  ```dset distance=mean;```
  
  ```dset NEGBRlen=prohibit;```
  
  ```dset rates=gamma```
  
  ```nj;```
  
  ```savetrees file=nj_morph_tree.tre format=Newick brlens=yes;```
