# Herbarium Lab
In this lab, we will be going around the trails of Skyline college to document bryophyte diversity and make herbarium vouchers of cryptogams for the Skyline herbarium.

## Learning Objectives
- Learning about the importance and use of natural history museums
- Learn how to identify cryptogams using a dichotomous key
- Learn how to press plants and make herbarium vouchers

## Pre-Lab
- Learn how to [collect and voucher a plant](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cawaLyoANkY)
- Learn about [the uniqueness of bryophytes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pBU3T2LZqfA)
- Learn how to [collect bryophytes in collecting packets](https://chapters.cnps.org/bryophyte/collecting/)

## Lab Procedures
1. We are going to hike around the Skyline College trails and collect mossess, lichen, liverworts, and other cryptogams for the Skyline Herbarium.
2. Collect your favorite moss, note the habitat and GPS location by taking a photo with your phone.
3. Using a dissecting scope, [identify your moss](https://herbariadb.berkeley.edu/archive/mossflora/general.html)
4. Store in a collecting packet
5. Record voucher information in our [skyline herbarium database](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ne1SpKAn2I3vnuQsNB6Ycyy8DUmrxru-ZTX8_inmi_E/edit?gid=0#gid=0), I will assign you a voucher number.
