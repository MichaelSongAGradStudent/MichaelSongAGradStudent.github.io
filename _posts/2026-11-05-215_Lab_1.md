# Algae and Protist Diversity Lab
In this lab, we will be learning about Eukaryotic diversity by studying organisms that are not plants, not animals, and not fungi. It turns out there are a lot of them! 

## Learning Objectives
- Learn how to use a compound microscope
- Learn how to read and create a dichotomous key
- Learn how to interpret a phylogeny
- Learn the major eukaryotic clades

## Pre-lab
1. Watch this video on [how to use a compound microscope](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUo2fHZaZCU)
2. Watch this video on [how to prepare a slide](https://youtube.com/shorts/TsM0hzz30v8?si=GGkg9IMIiHohOtDk)
3. Read this tutorial on [phylogenetics and how to read a tree](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/evolution-101/the-history-of-life-looking-at-the-patterns/understanding-phylogenies/)
4. Watch this video on [protist diversity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ln69k7LyTsU)
5. Watch this video on [how to use a dichotomous key](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uo6bF39KZkw)

## Lab procedures
- Look at the following organisms at 40x and 100x. Draw them and make notes of their structures, how they move, and how they acquire nutrients:
  1. _Euglena_, note the eye spot
  2. _Stentor_, note the micronucleus
  3. _Volvox_, note the colonies
  4. _Paramecium_, note the pellicle and oral groove
  5. _Amoeba_, note the pseudopodia and cytoplasmic streaming
  6. _Arcella_, note the test
  7. _Diatom_, note the test
  8. _Oscillatoria_, note the unique movement
  9. _Anabaena_, note the somatic, heterocyst, and akinetes
  10. _Clorella_
  11. _Spirostomum_
  12. _Chilomonas_
  13. _Foraminfera_ and _Radiolaria_ strew
  14. _Desmids_
  15. _Gonium_
  16. _Spirogyra_
  17. _Scenedesmus_
  18. _Nitella_/_Chara_
- Make a dichotomous key to identify these 18 taxa
- Using the internet, look up which major Eukaryotic clade each of these taxa are in (e.g. SAR, Excavata, Archaeplastida, Unikonta, although, there are two sneaky bacteria in here) and draw a phylogeny of all 18 taxa. 

## Assessment
- You will be responsible for IDing these 18 taxa in a **practicum** in three weeks, but you can use your dichotomous key!
- Show me your dichotomous key and phylogeny for 10 points.
