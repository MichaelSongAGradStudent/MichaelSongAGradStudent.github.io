# Nanopore Sequencing Lab
In this lab, we will be sequencing our PCR products on our nanopore sequencer! 

## Learning Objectives
- Learn how to perform library prep
- Learn how to perform Nanopore Sequencing

## Lab Materials
- MinION/GridION Flow Cell (DNA) FLO-MIN114, lead time 1 week, US$140.00
- Rapid Barcoding Sequencing Kit 24 V14 SQK-RBK114.24, US$140.00

## Pre-Lab
- Read the [library prep protocol](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YxO5ZwS7c7hgyBKxJd6mDzc6Gc6TmRuQmTHxda9r9Jw/edit?tab=t.0)
- Read the [sequencing protocol](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YxO5ZwS7c7hgyBKxJd6mDzc6Gc6TmRuQmTHxda9r9Jw/edit?tab=t.0)

## Lab Procedures
- Prepare the barcode library for the successful PCR products.
- Run the samples and retrieve the sequences for your samples.
- If successful, paste the sequences into the [herbarium database](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ne1SpKAn2I3vnuQsNB6Ycyy8DUmrxru-ZTX8_inmi_E/edit?usp=drive_web&ouid=102061328514094985941)

## Assessment
- If we get successful sequences, everyone gets 10 points!
