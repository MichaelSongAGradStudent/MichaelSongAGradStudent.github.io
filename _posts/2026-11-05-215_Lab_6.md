# Spore Counting Lab
In this lab, we will be determining ploidy level in plants using spore size and pollen size.
We will also be germinating spores to grow gametophytes.

## Learning Objectives
- Learn aseptic technique
- Learn how to determining ploidy using spore size
- Learn how to use a one-sided t-test
- Learn how to do plant cloning

## Pre-Lab
1. Walk around campus and pick a flower that is producing pollen and a fern or moss that is producing spores.
2. Bring a plastic food container with a lid.
3. Bring a plant you'd like to clone. 

## Lab Materials
1. Lots of peat moss for planting
2. Many different types of geraniums in flower
3. Sterilized nutrient media plated in Petri dishes
4. Sterilize water

## Lab Procedures

### Determining the ploidy of horticultural germaniums
I have bought several geraniums from Ace Hardware. We want to know the ploidy level of each cultivar.
According to [Cramer (1999)](https://journals.ashs.org/hort/hort/published/rest/pdf-watermark/v1/journals/horttech/9/4/article-p594.pdf/watermark-pdf/), the mean pollen size for diploid geraniums is 4.41µm and the mean pollen size for tetraploid geraniums is 5.9µm. The average number of germinal pores per pollen grain is 2.1 for diploids and 3.8 for tetraploids. 

1. Calibrate your eyepiece reticle with a stage micrometer.
2. Measure 6 pollen grains for each of the geraniums that I bought.
3. Use a t-test to determine the ploidy level of each variety. 

### Making a database of spore sizes for Skyline's plants
Now measure the spore size for the two species that you collected on Skyline's campus.

1. Use iNaturalist or the [Jespson key](https://herbariadb.berkeley.edu/eflora/keys/IJM_fam_key.php) to identify which species you collected.
2. Calibrate your eyepiece reticle with a stage micrometer.
3. Measure 10 pollen grains from the angiosperm and record the mean and standard deviation.
4. Measure 10 spores from the fern and record the mean and standard deviation.
5. Record the data in our [class spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/18v3Hu88GV8s85gjXMppHzI_cQBC0Csr10kxK27OZQqQ/edit?usp=sharing). 

### Germinating fern spores
We will now germinate your fern spores using aseptic technique to grow gametophytes. 

1. Wash a shallow plastic contain with a lid and soak it in a 10% bleach solution for 10 minutes. Dry.
2. Sterilize growth medium (peat moss) by placing moist medium in a microwave for 5 minutes, until steaming.
3. Put growth medium into the sterile container and sprinkle spores across.
4. Put translucent lid over or cover with plastic wrap and place under bright indirect light.
5. Come back in two to eight weeks to see if germination is successful. 

### Plant cloning
We will now try cloning some of our plants.

1. Sanitize your work station.
2. Sanitize all of your dissection tools (e.g., tweezers, razor blades). 
3. Collect some meristematic tissue like a root tip.
4. In a falcon tube, mix a 10% bleach solution with 2 drops of dish soap and the plant sample for 15 minutes.
5. Discard the solution and rinse with sterilized water 5 times.
6. Place plant onto Petri dish with sterilized nutrient media.
7. Store in growth chamber set to 25 degrees and 16 hours of light a day.
8. Come back in two to eight weeks to see if callus formation is successful.
