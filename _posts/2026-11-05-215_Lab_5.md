# Chromosome Squash Lab
In this lab, we will be performing chromosome squashes to count chromosomes and identify diploid and polyploid karyotypes.
We will be looking at both mitotic and meiotic squashes. 

## Learning Objectives
- Learn how to perform a chromosome squash

## Pre-Lab
- Watch how to perform a [meiotic chromosome squash](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xVV4qBfSQLs&t=2s)
- Watch how to perform a [mitotic chromosome squash](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=erUlHn6yheU)

## Materials
- 95% ethanol
- Concentrated HCl
- Acetocarmine Stain: 2g carmine in 100 ml 45% AcOH. Heat until warm, then boil for 5 minutes. Cool and filter.
- Slides and cover slips
- Blades

## Lab Procedures
Modified from [Root Tip Squash Procedueres from Parrot Lab](https://www.parrottlab.uga.edu/files/2024/08/Root-tip-squash-21.pdf)

### Mitotic Squash
Perform a mitotic squash of root tips of _Azolla_. How many chromosome are there? Take a photo of prophase, metaphase, anaphase, and telophase. 

1. Cut off a white tip of the root. Place tip on ice.
2. In a watch glass. Using a pipette, add three drops of 95% ethanol and one drop of HCl.
3. Add the root tip to the mixture for 5 seconds. Meristem will be white, rest will be translucent
4. Place root tip on a slide. Remove everything but the white. Blot off excess solution.
5. Add a drop of acetocarmine stain. Heat until warm on an alcohol lamp or a bunsen burner set to very low, do not boil.  
6. Dice will razor blade. Place cover slip over stained diced tip.
7. Place paper towel over slide and press until squished.
8. Look under a microscope. 

### Meiotic Squash
Perform a meiotic squash of anthers of the common yarrow. How many chromosome are there? 

1. Cut off a young anther. Place anther on ice.
2. In a watch glass. Using a pipette, add three drops of 95% ethanol and one drop of HCl.
3. Add the anther to the mixture for 5 seconds.
4. Place anther on a slide. Blot off excess solution.
5. Add a drop of acetocarmine stain. Heat until warm on an alcohol lamp or a bunsen burner set to very low, do not boil.  
6. Dice will razor blade. Place cover slip over stained diced tip.
7. Place paper towel over slide and press until squished.
8. Look under a microscope. 

## Assessment
Take a photo of each squash and show me for 10 points. 
1. How many chromosomes do you expect if the _Azolla_ were diploid? Is this _Azolla_ diploid?
2. How many chromosomes do you expect if the yarrow were diploid? Is this yarrow diploid?
