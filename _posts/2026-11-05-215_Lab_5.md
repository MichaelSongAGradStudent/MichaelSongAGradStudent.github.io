# Chromosome Squash Lab
In this lab, we will be performing chromosome squashes to count chromosomes and identify diploid and polyploid karyotypes.
We will be looking at both mitotic and meiotic squashes. 

## Learning Objectives
- Learn how to perform a chromosome squash

## Pre-Lab
- Watch how to perform a [meiotic chromosome squash](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xVV4qBfSQLs&t=2s)
- Watch how to perform a [mitotic chromosome squash](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=erUlHn6yheU)

## Materials
[Root Tip Squash Materials from Parrot Lab](https://www.parrottlab.uga.edu/files/2024/08/Root-tip-squash-21.pdf)

## Lab Procedures
[Root Tip Squash Procedueres from Parrot Lab](https://www.parrottlab.uga.edu/files/2024/08/Root-tip-squash-21.pdf)

### Mitotic Squash
Perform a mitotic squash of root tips of _Azolla_. How many chromosome are there? Take a photo of prophase, metaphase, anaphase, and telophase. 

### Meiotic Squash
Perform a meiotic squash of anthers of the California Poppy. How many chromosome are there? 

## Assessment
Take a photo of each squash and show me for 10 points.
